\section{Experimental Setup \& Benchmarking}
\label{sec:experimental_setup}

Having established the theoretical design and optimization of the \texttt{DetectorNX} architecture, both the \gls{snn} and its \gls{cnn} counterpart,
the final step is to formulate a rigorous evaluation strategy to empirically validate its performance against the continuous \gls{cnn} baseline.
In accordance with the project's primary objectives, the evaluation cannot be limited to a singular metric of bounding box accuracy; rather, it must holistically assess the neuromorphic paradigm.
To achieve this, a comprehensive benchmarking suite was designed (as mentioned in Figure \ref{fig:system_architecture_block}) comprising five distinct experimental protocols:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Dynamic Power Estimation
    \item Quantization Error Analysis (Accuracy vs. Object Size)
    \item Confidence Calibration
    \item Temporal Evidence Accumulation
    \item Real-time Inference latency
    \item Theoretical Energy Consumption
    \item Global Evaluator: Mean/Median \gls{iou}, Recall, and Average Detections Per Frame
\end{itemize}

This multi-faceted methodology ensures that the comparative findings are statistically robust and firmly grounded in the established literature surrounding event-based vision.
