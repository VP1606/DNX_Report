\section{Report Structure}
\label{sec:intro_report_structure}

The report is structured to first establish the necessary theoretical foundation, before detailing the architecture and its empirical evaluation:

\paragraph{Background.}
\noindent Chapter~\ref{cha:background} provides the prerequisite theory for the \texttt{DetectorNX} project. It establishes \gls{cnn} and \gls{snn} fundamentals, event-based vision, and neuromorphic energy economics, before concluding with related work to define the gap in high-definition automotive spiking detection that this project addresses.

\paragraph{Methodology.}
\noindent Chapter~\ref{cha:methodology} details the technical implementation: the \texttt{DetectorNX-G3-SNN} and \texttt{DetectorNX-G3-CNN} architectures, the shared \gls{bolt} preprocessing pipeline, the four-generation iterative design evolution, and the experimental setup.

\paragraph{Results and Evaluation.}
\noindent Chapter~\ref{cha:results_evaluation} presents the empirical evaluation against the \gls{cnn} baseline across accuracy, energy, latency, and confidence-calibration, with critical analysis of each finding.

\paragraph{Conclusions.}
\noindent Chapter~\ref{cha:summary_conclusions} closes the report by evaluating the project against the success criteria defined in Section~\ref{sec:intro_success_criteria}. It summarises the principal findings, reflects critically on experimental limitations, and outlines potential areas of future work.
