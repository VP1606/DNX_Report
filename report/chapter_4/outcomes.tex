\section{Summary of Outcomes}
\label{sec:summary_outcomes}

This project set out to determine whether the theoretical energy advantages of event-driven spiking dynamics could be harnessed for high-definition automotive perception,
whilst still retaining the spatial precision needed to be viable.

By enforcing strict architectural parity between \texttt{DetectorNX-G3-SNN} and a continuous baseline,
the empirical results decisively validate the neuromorphic paradigm for edge deployment in this domain.

The evaluation proved that the multi-box spiking architecture successfully mitigates the 1-bit
quantisation penalty, achieving near-parity on both geometric (\gls{miou}) and detection-quality (\gls{map_50}) metrics,
achieving $94.9\%$ and $91.3\%$ parity respectively.
Crucially, it delivered these results while demonstrating ``reactive scalability'',consuming
power only in direct proportion to scene activity,yielding an order-of-magnitude reduction in
theoretical energy consumption per frame. 
Despite the latency overhead of simulating \gls{lif} dynamics on synchronous \gls{gpu} hardware,
the spiking model maintained an $8.6\times$ safety margin above the real-time threshold, 
which confirms its immediate viability for \gls{adas} even on conventional \gls{gpu} hardware.

\vspace{1em}
In summary, while the absolute spatial precision (\gls{miou} $\approx 0.46$) of the \texttt{G3}
architecture remains below the threshold required for immediate deployment in safety-critical
\gls{adas} systems, the empirical evidence demonstrates that \texttt{DetectorNX-G3-SNN} has
successfully bridged the precision gap \textit{relative} to an equivalent continuous baseline. By
definitively proving the reactive energy scalability and temporal reliability of the spiking
paradigm at high-definition, this research transitions \glspl{snn} from a theoretical curiosity
into a rigorously quantified architectural alternative, while exposing the areas of weakness, such as temporal quantisation and detection head mismatches, that
future work must resolve to achieve production-grade automotive perception.
