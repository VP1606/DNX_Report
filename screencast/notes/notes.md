# Screencast Notes

## Format

- Duration: 7-9 minutes.
- Audience: Typical 3rd Year CS Students without specific project knowledge.
- Structure: Beginning + middle + end: intro -> main -> conclusion.
- Marker will watch this BEFORE paper.
- imagine you're a small company producing an online sales video promoting your work — the marker is interested in the technical achievements, but you're selling them on it.

## Criteria

### §4.1: Demonstrating Achievements

```
(90+)Precise/concise account of achievements supported by theory; very clear
```

**Implication for our content:** the screencast needs an elevator pitch, a setup of the problem (regulatory pressure, dense CNN energy cost), a clear statement of what was built (DetectorNX), and the headline results (94.9% mIoU parity, 94.33% energy saving, calibration finding). All grounded in why SNNs make sense.

### §4.2: Use of the Medium

```
(90+)Audio + visuals fuse into a seamless whole; entertaining; close to professional production
```

Implication: slide-with-voiceover gets you to Expected. To break into Above (70+) you need a creative element — could be visualisations of SNN dynamics, animated diagrams from the report, screen-capture of the model running on event data, B-roll of automotive scenes, or background music. Editing is essential — no awkward pauses, consistent audio levels.

### §4.3: Audience

What it scores: appropriateness of pitch and technical level for a third-year CS student who hasn't done SNNs.

```
Utterly compelling for the target audience
```

Implication: the marker knows CNNs, knows backprop, knows object detection — but probably doesn't know LIF dynamics, surrogate gradients, ETraM, neuromorphic hardware, or Reactive Scalability. The screencast must define these (briefly) before using them, but also leverage what the audience does know (CNN object detection as a baseline frame of reference).

### §4.4: Structure & Flow

What it scores: structural quality of the presentation — beginning/middle/end coherence, narrative flow, pace, clarity of speech.

```
Viewer is sad the screencast ended
```

Implication: the narrative arc has to be tight. Beginning hooks the viewer (the legislation/energy-cost framing from the abstract works well here). Middle delivers method + key results. End synthesises and points forward.
